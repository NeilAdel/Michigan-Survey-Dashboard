6. Question routing checks

Eligibility is operationalized as a direction response of “Go up” (1) or “Go down” (5). Magnitude responses are counted after special codes are removed.

These are empirical consistency checks, not proof of the official questionnaire routing. Verify the questionnaire wording and skip instructions before interpreting non-eligible magnitude responses.
